About Tarot Tool

Welcome to TarotTool. What started as a learning exercise has grown into a multi-feature web app. TarotTool allows you to log and explore your readings over time. View trends and patterns in both the cards and the questions you ask, and dig deeper into your Tarot practice

About the creator

I'm Alex, a UBC Okanagan Computer Science graduate currently based in Vancouver, BC. I've always had an interest in tarot cards as a tool for introspection and mindfullness practice, and I was often frustrated trying to keep track of my readings in a paper journal. Creating a digital tool to solve that problem was the natural next step.

Check out my Github to see what else I'm working on.
